Digital twin accuracy vs the plant (measured live)

Every 5 s the twin forecasts pH and chlorine 10/30/60 simulated minutes ahead; when the time comes it's scored against the plant's sensors. The simulated plant deliberately differs from the twin (different feed strength and chlorine decay, drifting demand, sensor noise). Forecasts are skipped if someone changes a setpoint before they come due.
